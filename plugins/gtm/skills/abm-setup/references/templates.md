# Templates

## Leadership or board ask (one slide)
```
Executive summary
- Sales-led traction is real; marketing's share of it is not yet measurable.
- Our headline <metric> is <X>% self-serve. It measures product-led volume, not enterprise pipeline.
- Top risk: until contribution is measurable, marketing spend cannot be tied to revenue.
- Ask: endorse ABM as the GTM operating model, with signal-based target-account KPIs
  replacing volume as marketing's yardstick.
- For awareness: <bounded experiment, e.g. a list-based paid test this quarter>.
```
