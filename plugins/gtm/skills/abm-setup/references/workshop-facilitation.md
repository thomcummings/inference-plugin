# Kickoff workshop

A two-hour working session that turns "we're doing ABM" into shared definitions, a first tiering and next steps. Run it on a live shared board, so every edit persists and the board becomes v0 of the command center.

## Prep (1 week before)
- Assemble a candidate account pool: CRM companies, the market map, current deals, product sign-ups from company domains. Score each account with the ICP score.
- Pre-place each account's current stage from the CRM or list status.
- Prepare default stage cards (`abm-strategy` `references/funnel-stages.md`), the play library (`abm-strategy` `references/plays-and-systems.md`) and starter clusters. Use the `abm-strategy` design if one exists.
- Pick one worked-example account that's stalled, to make the session concrete.

## Attendees
Founders or execs, sales, partnerships, solutions engineering, marketing, and developer or community relations where the company has them. Seven or eight people is ideal.

## Agenda

| Time | Step | What happens | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 min | Why ABM, why now | Readiness test, the volume-vs-enterprise slide, the inverted funnel, failure modes | Shared rationale |
| 25 min | 1. Define the funnel | Walk the six stage cards (entry, exit, job). Edit them live and mark each one agreed. Debate Aware vs Engaged. | Stage definitions, some locked |
| 25 min | 2. Map accounts | Board with six stage columns. Drag accounts into their stage. Mark uncertain placements (dashed). Flag whether sales and marketing each know the account. | Accounts placed with confidence marks |
| 25 min | 3. Prioritize | Fit × readiness matrix with draggable split lines. Tiers appear live, and a warning shows past the Tier 1 cap. | Draft Tier 1, 2 and 3 |
| 25 min | 4. Plan | For Tier 1: a plan sheet per account (target stage, date, aim, trigger, message, owner, buying-group roles, sales and marketing steps). For Tier 2: programs on a stage × tier grid by cluster. | First plans and programs |
| 5 min | Close | Owners and dates for the next steps | Action list |

Run the worked example during step 4: map its buying committee and brainstorm plays at every stage.

## Board features that help
- A four-step header: Define → Map → Prioritize → Plan.
- Filters by segment and cluster on every step after Define.
- Stage cards that can be edited and locked ("agreed").
- Stage columns with drag-and-drop, plus an "uncertain" style for placements the team isn't sure about.
- A matrix with draggable split lines, a configurable Tier 1 cap and a live warning.
- Plan sheets per account, play suggestions by stage, and an ideas list.
- A program grid of stages × tiers, with cluster tags and starter programs.
- Shared persistence (everyone's edits saved), with a clear read-only state for viewers.

## Standard next steps to close on
1. Review, tier and refine the target list. Owners: sales lead + ABM owner. Due within 2 weeks.
2. Lock stage criteria, especially Aware vs Engaged. Owners: ABM owner + sales lead.
3. Set marketing stage-movement goals once the list is set. Owner: marketing lead.
4. Second iteration of the board as a weekly pipeline and progress view on top of the CRM. Owner: ABM owner.
5. Log every target-account touch in the CRM, for attribution. Owner: sales lead.
6. Marketing joins the weekly account review.
7. Add buying-committee questions to first sales calls.
8. Map the last 10 blog posts to segment, persona and stage, and brief the missing procurement and security piece.
9. Evaluate website de-anonymization.
10. Scope paid budget for Unaware accounts, and re-assess events against ABM goals.
11. Share the ABM approach with the wider company.

## After the session
- Write a summary page within 48 hours: agreed direction, session detail, next steps with owners, and open questions.
- Store it with the other workshop pages, if this sits inside a wider GTM offsite.
- Record the decisions: ABM as the operating model, CRM as the system of record, the stage model.
