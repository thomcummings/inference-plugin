# 12-week rollout

| Weeks | Focus | Deliverables | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Case (`abm-strategy`) | Readiness test, volume-vs-enterprise analysis, leadership ask | ABM endorsed as the operating model; ABM owner named |
| 1 | Workshop | Prep pool and scores; 2-hour workshop; summary page | Draft stages, tiers and plans; next steps with owners |
| 2–3 | Design and list | Stage criteria locked; ICP rubric written; Tier 1 vs 2 criteria written; list in one source; sync to the CRM property and segments | List reviewed by sales; Tier 1 within the cap; stage mapping in the glossary |
| 2–3 | Rhythm | Sales weekly renamed to ABM weekly, with marketing in it; first monthly recalibration scheduled | Two weekly meetings held, each ending with top actions |
| 3–5 | Command center v1 | Board on the list + CRM deal stages; suggested and confirmed stages; owners; next steps; notes | Team uses it in the weekly meeting |
| 4–6 | Signals v2 | Web visitor identification live; weekly signals; contacts; usage; weekly review view | Signals discussed in the meeting; coverage reported |
| 5–8 | Channels | Content audit and procurement/security piece; list-based paid live; events re-scored; relations goal set; launch pre-briefs | Each channel has an ABM goal and owner |
| 6–9 | Habits v3 | Tags + digests; next-step reminders; next steps → CRM tasks; this quarter's wins view; multiple owners | Overdue steps trend down |
| 8–12 | Measurement | North star and driver tree on the GTM dashboard; definitions agreed; attribution level stated; first stage-movement goals tracked | Quarterly review uses account movement as the headline |
| 10–12 | Intelligence v4 (optional) | Visits view; tracker issues; win likelihood | Used in at least one weekly decision |

**Gates:** don't build reporting before stages are locked. Don't automate signals before the list is in one source. Don't add chat posts the team won't read.

**Handover to `abm-ops`:** once two ABM weeklies have run with top actions recorded, the list syncs from one source and the command center v1 is in use, the programme is live. From there the weekly meeting, monthly recalibration and health checks are `abm-ops`; the later rollout rows continue alongside it.
