# Re-pointing channels at the list

## Content
- **Audit:** map the last ~10 pieces to segment × persona × funnel stage. Most teams find almost everything targets developers or technical leads.
- **Fill the gaps in this order:**
  1. Procurement and security (data handling, deployment options, compliance).
  2. CTO and economic buyer (business case, cost of the problem).
  3. Cluster-specific proof (a case study per cluster).
- **Repackage before creating.** Cut existing proof (benchmarks, studies, stories) by cluster.
- Content written for one account usually works for its cluster and for similar self-serve visitors. ABM and self-serve content aren't mutually exclusive.
- Keep one brand voice, and flex tone and channel by audience.
- Hold a weekly content planning slot that takes requests from the ABM weekly.

## Paid
- The role of paid is to reach Unaware accounts at scale. Use list-based audiences × buying-group roles, not broad targeting.
- Agency choice: decide between a broad multi-channel test optimized to self-serve activation (light on ABM) and a specialist list-based social ABM partner (deep on one channel, needs sales to act fast and a steady supply of creative). State the trade-off explicitly. Either way, report target-account reach separately from self-serve results.
- Before spend: agree one target list with the agency, fix tracking and UTMs (paid social tagged as paid), and send the funnel numbers that set the pass mark.
- A thin list-based budget won't reach enough people per account. Stress-test reach per account.

## Events
- Every event serves one of two jobs, with separate scorecards: **pipeline** (target-account buying groups) or **mindshare** (the practitioner community). When budget is tight, pipeline wins.
- Pipeline formats: curated dinners around conferences (built from named accounts first), account dinners in your home city, expert audits or lunch-and-learns at the account, an invite-only practitioner council.
- Log attendance by account in the CRM and the command center, and name a follow-up owner per event.
- Kill line: if dinners don't move target accounts within two quarters, change the format or stop.

## Developer, community and partner relations
- Where the company has DevRel or a community team, make finding people at target accounts an explicit goal: sign-ups from target domains, community members, docs readers.
- Partnerships and sales cover the commercial relationship; relations teams cover the practitioners. Align goals quarterly.

## Launches
- Target accounts hear first: a personal note before each public launch explaining why it matters to them.
- Brief account owners with a one-liner per Tier 1 account.

## Website
- Add vertical or cluster landing pages to test messaging for target accounts.
- Route high-intent visits (pricing, proof pages, docs) from listed domains to owners within a day.
