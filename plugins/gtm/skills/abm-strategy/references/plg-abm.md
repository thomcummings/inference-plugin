# The PLG–ABM hybrid

Account-based and product-led are not opposites; mature companies run them
together. The bridge is **product-led sales (PLS)**.

## Product-led sales (PLS)
A self-serve front end (freemium / free trial) for acquisition, converted and
expanded by a sales-assisted or sales-led motion — distinct from pure PLG (no
sales touch) and from classic sales-led (no self-serve). It's the path companies
like Slack, Atlassian and Figma took as they matured out of pure PLG: the product
generates the signal and the qualified accounts; sales works the biggest of them
account-by-account.

For an ABM context, PLS is how product usage becomes the intent engine feeding
the account programme.

## PQA vs PQL — keep these distinct
The critical distinction for a PLG–ABM hybrid:

- **PQL — product-qualified *lead*.** An *individual user* whose in-product
  behaviour marks them as a good prospect (hit an activation milestone, invited
  teammates, hit a usage/paywall limit). Person-level.
- **PQA — product-qualified *account*.** The *company/account* showing aggregate
  product-qualified behaviour — multiple users, spreading usage, a team taking
  hold. Account-level, and therefore the unit that plugs into ABM tiering and
  buying-group work.

In a hybrid motion you roll PQLs up into PQAs: several engaged users inside one
org become a product-qualified *account* that gets tiered, mapped (who are these
users in the buying group?), and worked with plays — often to expand from a
bottom-up foothold to a top-down enterprise deal.

## How it changes the ABM build
- **Selection:** product usage becomes a top-tier signal (often the strongest);
  PQAs jump the tiering queue.
- **Buying group:** existing users are known champions/end-users already inside
  the DMU — map outward from them to the economic buyer and blockers.
- **Plays:** land-and-expand plays triggered by usage thresholds; combine
  bottom-up product signals with top-down account outreach.
- **Measurement:** usage-based account health sits alongside the standard
  account-level metrics (`references/measurement.md`).

## When it applies
Only when the product has a genuine self-serve/freemium surface generating usage
data. For pure sales-led products with no self-serve, ignore PQA/PQL and run the
standard signal set. If the growth-model question of whether to *add* a
product-led motion is live, that's `growth-model`, not this skill.
