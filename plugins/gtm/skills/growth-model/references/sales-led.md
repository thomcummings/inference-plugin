# Sales-led (and sales-assisted inbound)

**What it is.** Humans close the deal; marketing's job is to create and feed
qualified pipeline. **Sales-led** drives demand outbound/account-based and sells
through a rep; **sales-assisted inbound** lets marketing/content/product generate
interest, then a human converts it. The compounding lives in brand, content, and
reputation that lower CAC over time — not in the sales act itself.

**When it fits.** Higher ACV that pays for a human; a buyer who expects to be sold
to (procurement, security, exec sponsor); a considered, multi-stakeholder purchase;
a product whose value needs framing or configuration to land. Most B2B with ACV
above the self-serve ceiling lands here or in PLS.

**When it doesn't.** Low ACV that can't carry sales cost (the unit economics break —
this wants PLG/self-serve); a tiny or undefined ICP where reps burn time on bad-fit
accounts; a pre-PMF product where "more pipeline" papers over weak pull. Scaling reps
before the motion is proven is the classic premature-scale error.

**Engine shape.**
- Engine: repeatable pipeline generation → qualified opportunities → closed-won, with
  brand/content/referrals lowering CAC over time.
- Fuel: target accounts, qualified meetings, sales capacity.
- Lubricants: ICP sharpness, messaging/sales enablement, proof, win/loss learning,
  conversion at each stage.
- Boosts: events, webinars, PR spikes. Reinvestment: reputation, references and
  content that compound into cheaper future pipeline.

**Fit tests that bite.** Channel-model fit (does ACV/payback support the sales cost?);
ICP fit (is the target list tight enough that reps aren't wasted?); team/founder fit
(can they actually sell, or run reps?); stage fit (is the motion proven before
hiring more reps?).

**Decay signals.** CAC/payback degrading; win rates falling; sales cycle lengthening;
rising "lost to no-decision"; pipeline increasingly bought (paid) rather than earned
(brand/referral); reps added faster than the proven motion supports.

**Lineage.** Classic B2B GTM; Growth Unhinged / OpenView on sales-assist and PLS;
April Dunford (positioning as the input that makes the sales pitch land); win/loss
practice.
