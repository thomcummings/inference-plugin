# Founder-led sales / founder-led growth

**What it is.** The founder is the growth engine: selling, posting, building
relationships, doing the first dozens of deals or building the first audience by
hand. Not a permanent model — a *deliberate, evidence-generating phase* that earns
the right to a repeatable motion. Often the honest answer pre- and early-PMF, even
when the deck says PLG.

**When it fits.** Pre-PMF or early-PMF; small number of deals where learning matters
more than scale; a market where the founder's credibility, network or audience opens
doors; before there's enough signal to know what a repeatable motion even is. The
founder's unfair advantages (story, relationships, taste) are the channel.

**When it doesn't.** When it's used to *avoid* building a repeatable motion (the
founder becomes the permanent bottleneck and growth caps at their personal capacity);
when the founder genuinely can't or won't do customer-facing work; when the business
is past the point where hand-to-hand makes sense and needs a system.

**Engine shape.**
- Engine: the founder's direct effort and credibility → deals/audience → learning →
  the inputs for a repeatable motion.
- Fuel: founder time and attention (the scarcest, least scalable fuel).
- Lubricants: sharper ICP and message learned from each conversation; a light CRM/
  notes loop so learning compounds.
- Boosts: a viral post, a podcast hit. Reinvestment: every deal/conversation sharpens
  ICP, positioning and the eventual playbook.

**Fit tests that bite.** Founder/team fit (will they actually do it, with energy?);
stage fit (is hand-to-hand right for now, or is it avoidance of building a system?);
the exit question — what signal says it's time to hand off to a repeatable motion?

**Decay signals.** Growth flattening at the founder's personal capacity; the founder
as permanent single point of failure; learning no longer compounding (same
conversations, no new insight) — the cue to graduate to PLG/sales-led/etc.

**Lineage.** YC canon (do things that don't scale; founder-led sales); Sean Ellis /
Vohra (use the phase to find must-have signal before scaling); Inference doctrine
(this maps to the advisor's "founder-led learning before scaling channels").
