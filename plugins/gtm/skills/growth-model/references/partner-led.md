# Partner / channel / white-label motions

**What it is.** Growth flows through other companies — resellers, agencies,
integration/marketplace partners, or white-label embeds — who put your product in
front of their customers. The partner's distribution becomes your channel; the loop
is "more partners and more partner success → more end customers → more attractive to
partners."

**When it fits.** A product that complements an established platform's offering
(integration/marketplace pull); a buyer already served by agencies/resellers who
trust an intermediary; a white-label opportunity where a platform wants your
capability as their feature (e.g. an HR platform adding hardware, payroll adding
benefits); economics that survive a partner's margin. Strong when the partner gets
stickier or richer by carrying you.

**When it doesn't.** Pre-PMF (you can't ask a partner to sell what you haven't proven
— their reputation is on the line); thin margins that can't fund a partner cut; no
clear "what's in it for them" (ARR, stickiness, completeness); reliance on a single
partner who can switch you off (concentration risk). Partner motions also have long
lead times — not a quick-win channel.

**Engine shape.**
- Engine: partner recruitment + partner enablement → partner-sourced customers →
  proof that recruits more partners.
- Fuel: target partners, enablement assets, co-marketing.
- Lubricants: onboarding partners, making them successful fast, clear economics,
  co-selling support.
- Boosts: a marquee partner signing. Reinvestment: each successful partner is proof
  and referral for the next; embeds compound as the partner's own growth carries you.

**Fit tests that bite.** Channel-model fit (do the economics survive the partner
margin?); product-motion fit (can a partner actually sell/embed it without you?);
stage fit (is the product proven enough to put a partner's name on?); concentration
risk (one partner or many?).

**Decay signals.** Partner-sourced volume concentrating in one partner; partner
activation/enablement stalling (signed but not selling); margin pressure as partners
demand more; the partner building or buying their own alternative.

**Lineage.** Classic channel/partnerships GTM; ecosystem-led growth (Crossbeam, nearbound
thinking); the white-label pattern seen in HR/fintech platforms adding adjacent
products as second revenue streams.
