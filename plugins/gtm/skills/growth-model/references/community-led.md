# Community-led growth

**What it is.** A community of users/practitioners becomes the engine: members
attract members, create content and proof, support each other, and pull new users in.
Growth compounds through belonging and peer activity rather than through ads or reps.
A genuine loop when it works — and a graveyard of dead Slack groups when it doesn't.

**When it fits.** A product or category with a shared identity or practice people
want to belong to; users who benefit from each other (peer learning, networking,
status); a topic with ongoing conversation, not a one-off transaction; a team
willing to invest patiently — community compounds slowly and dies if treated as a
campaign. Often pairs with content-led and PLG.

**When it doesn't.** No natural reason for users to talk to each other; a purely
transactional product; an expectation of fast returns (community is a long game);
no internal owner with genuine care — half-built communities actively signal
weakness. "Let's start a Slack" is not a community strategy.

**Engine shape.**
- Engine: member activity → content, proof, peer support, identity → attracts and
  retains more members.
- Fuel: engaged members, prompts, rituals, recognition.
- Lubricants: onboarding into the community, surfacing the best content, status
  mechanics, moderation.
- Boosts: launches, events, AMAs. Reinvestment: member-generated content and
  referrals pull the next cohort; community deepens retention.

**Fit tests that bite.** ICP fit (do these users actually want peer connection?);
team/founder fit (is there a patient, credible owner?); stage fit (is there enough
of a base to seed momentum, or is it too early?); data fit (can you see engagement,
not just headcount?).

**Decay signals.** Engagement concentrating in a shrinking core; posting becoming
mostly the company, not members; new-member activation falling; the community
becoming a support queue rather than a growth loop.

**Lineage.** Community-led growth canon (CMX, Lenny on community); Reforge on
engagement loops; contrast with audience/content-led (a community is participatory,
an audience is broadcast).
