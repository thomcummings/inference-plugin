## Monthly recalibration checklist
- [ ] Tier 1 count within the cap; rank Tier 1
- [ ] Promote Tier 3 accounts with signals; demote or retire stalled accounts (with reasons)
- [ ] Cluster changes
- [ ] Stage velocity: accounts stuck more than N weeks
- [ ] Buying-group coverage gaps
- [ ] Programs: keep, change or kill
- [ ] List sync verified (CRM property, segments, ad and visitor lists match the source)
