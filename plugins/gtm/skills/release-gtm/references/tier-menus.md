# Tier menus — instantiate, don't dump

Menus to **choose from** for each tier, not checklists to reproduce whole. The tier sets
the *breadth* and *investment*; the client's own traction (`evergreen/channels/` / the
channel-map picture) sets *which* channels actually get used. A T1 with no working paid
channel does not suddenly buy paid; spend where there's proven pull.

Every asset is pre-flagged **[A]** agent-draftable or **[H]** human-led. "Agent-draftable"
means an agent produces the first draft and variants cheaply; a human still owns the
value-prop phrasing and the final call. This is where the agentic dividend shows up — but
argue it against real capacity, don't assume the team can ship every [A] at once.

The menus below are software-native (changelog, in-app, ProductHunt, dev creators). For
services, physical, or regulated products, swap per `anchoring-and-cadence.md` — keep the
breadth, change the surface.

### Deriving the channel mix (do this, don't pick from the menu blind)

The channel mix is **derived from `channel-map` / `evergreen/channels/` first** — the
release doesn't invent a channel strategy, it spends the tier's breadth against the routes
already proven to work for *this* client. The menus below are candidates, not a checklist.

To avoid tunnelling onto the obvious owned/paid routes, run a quick **completeness check
across channel categories** — for each, ask "is this relevant to *this* release and
audience, and what does channel-map say about it?" Include, don't default to:

- **Owned** — site, email, in-app, changelog, blog.
- **Paid** — only where channel-map shows it already converts.
- **Earned** — PR, community, and credible creator/practitioner demonstration (this is the
  category that's *adjacent to the "powered by" proof channel* — someone trusted showing
  the product rather than the brand asserting it). Earned reach is not owned: if it's in
  the mix it needs named people seeded before launch, not hoped for.
- **Amplification** — team/employee socials, partner/investor networks. The cheapest reach
  you have; usually under-used.
- **Concentrated third-party moments** — launch directories (ProductHunt / HN),
  marketplaces, events. A one-off *boost*, not an engine — plan the day and the
  follow-through, don't expect compounding.

The output should show *why each chosen channel is in* (the channel-map signal) and, where
it matters, *why an obvious one is out*. Relevance is a judgement per release — nothing on
this list is mandatory, and channels not yet active can still be chosen if channel-map
shows them adjacent and worth a first test.

## T1 — new-to-world, category-shaping

**Goal:** net-new demand + move the positioning. **Lead time:** ~6–8 weeks. **Investment:** high.

**Channels (full mix, if traction supports each):** hero/anchor content asset · PR /
press · launch-directory moment (ProductHunt / HN) · dedicated launch page · webinar or
live event · email (announce + nurture) · paid (only where it already works) · organic
social run · **team/employee socials** · **credible creator / practitioner seeding
(adjacent to "powered by")** · in-app · changelog · founder / exec amplification.

**Asset checklist:**
- Hero asset — launch narrative / vision piece **[H]** (agent drafts supporting sections **[A]**)
- Launch landing page copy **[A]** → phrasing polished **[H]**
- Press release / press kit + briefing doc **[A]**, spokesperson quotes **[H]**
- Announcement email + nurture follow-ups **[A]**
- Demo video / product walkthrough script **[A]**, on-camera **[H]**
- Social run: teaser → launch → proof posts, multi-variant **[A]**
- Paid ad variants (if paid is a live channel) **[A]**
- Sales / CS enablement deck + FAQ + objection handling **[A]** → validated **[H]**
- Team-social amplification kit (pre-written posts + assets) **[A]**
- Creator-seeding brief + early-access list **[A]** draft, **[H]** owns the relationships
- ProductHunt / HN assets: gallery, first comment, hunter lined up **[A]** → **[H]** final
- Changelog + in-app announcement **[A]**

**Rollout countdown (work back from launch day):**
- Beta / early access — messaging fine-tuning + testimonial sourcing (real stage)
- L−4wk: narrative + message house locked; assets in draft
- L−2wk: press briefings under embargo; internal enablement live
- L−1wk: dry run; sales/CS/support briefed
- Launch day: coordinated fire across the mix
- L+: squeeze begins (see anchoring-and-cadence + SKILL step 5)

**Internal launch — hit list (each item earns its place with a reason):**

| Item | Why it's on the list |
|---|---|
| Sales brief: what it is, who it's for, the one-liner, the demo | Reps describe it consistently instead of improvising |
| Objection-handling / FAQ | Kills the "I don't know, let me check" that stalls deals |
| CS / support note + known-issues list | Support isn't blindsided; churn-risk questions answered |
| Exec / founder talking points | The loudest voices say the same thing publicly |
| #amplify kit (posts + assets, one-click) | Team socials actually fire instead of being a chore |
| Internal demo / walkthrough | Nobody sells what they haven't seen work |

First-class, not an afterthought — a T1 nobody internal can articulate under-fires.

## T2 — strong feature, mostly expansion

**Goal:** adoption + some acquisition. **Lead time:** ~3–4 weeks. **Investment:** medium.

**Channels:** landing page or updated feature page · email · blog post · in-app message
/ tour · organic social · **team/employee socials** · *optional:* launch-directory moment
(ProductHunt / HN) and a little **creator seeding** where the audience warrants · light
enablement · changelog.

**Asset checklist:**
- Feature / landing page copy **[A]** → phrasing polished **[H]**
- Announcement email **[A]**
- Blog post / deep-dive **[A]**
- In-app message + short tour copy **[A]**
- Social posts (2–4, variant) **[A]**
- Short demo clip script **[A]**
- Team-social amplification kit **[A]**
- Light sales/CS note: what shipped, who it's for, one-liner **[A]** → checked **[H]**
- Changelog entry **[A]**

**Rollout countdown:**
- Optional beta for testimonials if the surface allows
- L−2wk: message house + page in draft
- L−1wk: internal note out; assets ready
- Launch day: page + email + blog + in-app + social
- L+1–2wk: squeeze (repurpose the deep-dive into social, source quotes)

**Internal launch — hit list (lighter than T1, still real):**

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Crisp internal note: what / why / who-for / one-liner | One shared description, no freelancing |
| #amplify nudge with ready posts | Team socials fire on launch day |
| One-line note to sales/CS | They can field the "what's new?" question |

Not a full deck, but not silence either.

## T3 — useful improvement

**Goal:** adoption, light awareness. **Lead time:** ~1–2 weeks. **Investment:** low.

**Channels:** changelog · in-app · one blog or one social post.

**Asset checklist:**
- Changelog entry **[A]**
- In-app message **[A]**
- One social post or one short blog note **[A]**
- (Optional) one line in the next email roundup **[A]**

**Rollout:** ship it, note it, one amplification touch. Consider **bundling** several T3s
under one themed moment so they compound rather than dribbling out singly (see
anchoring-and-cadence).

**Enablement:** a one-liner in the team channel. No deck.

---

## T4 — fix / parity / minor

**Goal:** quiet credit. **Lead time:** none. **Investment:** minimal.

**Asset checklist:**
- Changelog entry **[A]**. That's it.

**Rollout:** none. Don't manufacture a moment. Roll it into the next roundup or theme if
it's worth a mention at all. Over-launching T4s is exactly the "app that cried wolf"
failure the tiering exists to prevent.

---

## The bundling move (applies across tiers)

Several small releases (T3/T4, sometimes T2s that lost the ranking) can be packaged under
one theme and launched as a single, larger moment — a "spring release", a themed roundup,
a "what's new in X" set. Bundling turns scattered low-tier ships into one compounding
chapter and protects the cadence guardrail (≤1 T1, 2–3 T2s per period). Reach for it
whenever the roadmap ranking produces a pile-up of items that each want a moment they
can't individually justify.
