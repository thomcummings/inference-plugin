# Finding material

Where stories hide, and what to ask when no context store covers it. Ask one to
three questions at a time, each with a suggested answer drawn from what you've
read, so the person can confirm or correct in a word. "None" is always a fair
answer.

## Sources to read, in order

1. **A context store**, if one exists: Start here, then proof (results,
   customer stories, numbers), story (company overview, origin), team
   (people and spokespeople), messaging and point of view (beliefs),
   decisions (recent calls and reversals), competitive (what others are doing).
2. **Systems of record the store links to**: ask for current numbers rather
   than copying them in. Usage, customer counts and revenue go in only with
   the person's say-so.
3. **The working folder**: decks, board updates, roadmaps, release notes,
   customer interviews, win-loss notes.
4. **The outside scan** (if web access exists): trade press, communities,
   competitors' recent news, regulation, seasonal moments.

## Interview prompts by source

**Product and roadmap**
- What has shipped in the last six months that changed what a customer can do?
- What's coming in the next 90 days, and how firm are the dates?
- What did you remove, reverse or decide not to build?

**Customers**
- Which customer result would you most like a stranger to hear about?
- Which customers would agree to be named, and to speak?
- Is there a customer who's interesting in their own right?

**Data**
- What do you know from your product data that your customers don't?
- What number surprised the team this year?
- Could any of it be published, anonymised and with permission?

**Partners**
- Who are you working with that people wouldn't expect?
- What can customers do now that they couldn't before the partnership?

**People**
- Who in the company has standing to speak publicly, and on what?
- Any notable hires, or a team doing something unusual?
- What's the honest origin story?

**Beliefs**
- What do you believe about your market that most of it doesn't?
- What industry habit do you think is wrong, and can you show it?

**Milestones**
- Funding, expansion, customer or revenue moments in the window? Which numbers
  are you willing to publish?

## Confidentiality

Mark every inventory item with one of: **public**, **can be made public**
(needs a sign-off, say whose), **embargoed until** a date, or **internal only**.
Internal-only items can still inform an angle (they explain the why) but can't
appear in a hook or as proof.
