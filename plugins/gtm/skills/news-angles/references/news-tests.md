# News tests

How to grade an angle. Run all six tests, then give a verdict. The tests adapt
classic news values (Galtung and Ruge; Harcup and O'Neill) to company stories.

## The six tests

Score each 0, 1 or 2. Write one line of reasoning for each; the reasoning is
what the person learns from.

| Test | Question | 0 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **New** | Is it new to the world, not just to the company? | Only new to us | A first, a reversal, or not known before |
| **Matters** | Does it change something for people outside the company? | Only affects us | Clear stakes for a named group |
| **Surprise** | Does it cut against what people expect? | Exactly what you'd expect | Contradicts a common belief |
| **Peg** | Is there a reason to tell it now? | Could run any time, or never | Tied to a running story, a date or a moment |
| **Proof** | Can it be shown, not just claimed? | Assertion only | Data, a named customer, a demo, a document |
| **Voice** | Is there someone with standing to tell it? | No spokesperson | A credible person, available and willing |

## Verdicts

- **News**: total 8 or more, with New or Surprise at 2 and Proof at least 1.
  Worth taking to press.
- **Feature**: strong on Matters, Surprise and Proof but Peg at 0. A good story
  waiting for a reason to run. Note the kind of peg to watch for.
- **Announcement**: New is 0 or Matters is 0. Real, worth saying, but on owned
  channels (site, newsletter, customers, social), not to press.
- **Not yet**: would be news or a feature, but Proof or Voice is 0. Name what
  would close the gap.

The numbers are a prompt for judgement, not a formula. When the score and your
read disagree, give your read and say why. A score of 9 with no peg is still a
feature.

## The outside test

Before scoring, write the hook as a reporter would, in one sentence, without
the company's product name. If the sentence only makes sense to the company,
the angle fails New or Matters regardless of the scores.

- Inside: "Pennant launches SlotSwap, its new AI-powered rebooking feature."
- Outside: "Vet clinics are letting pet owners trade appointment slots with
  each other."

## Common downgrades

- A competitor already did it: New drops to 0 unless the angle is a reversal
  or a better result.
- The proof depends on a customer who hasn't agreed: Proof is 0 until they do.
- The spokesperson is the only person who believes it: Voice is 1 at most.
- The peg is the company's own launch date: Peg is 1 at most. Calendars belong
  to the company, not the reader.
