# Stage and motion priors

These are the opinions the Stage lens applies. They are priors, not rules: when a client's
evidence disagrees with a prior, the evidence wins — but flag the divergence in the map,
because it's either a real edge worth amplifying or a measurement artefact worth chasing.

Use them hardest in evidence-thin mode, where they carry The Call almost alone.

## Cross-cutting priors (any motion)

- **Concentration beats coverage.** Two or three channels run properly outperform eight run
  thinly. The failure mode of most channel documents is licensing breadth; the map's job is
  to argue it back down.
- **Attribution before acquisition spend.** If most signups carry no source data, fixing
  attribution outranks launching anything new — every later decision inherits the gap.
- **Tune before launch.** A channel with proven reach and broken conversion is usually a
  better bet than a brand-new channel: half the loop already works.
- **Trust plumbing is cheap and chronically skipped.** Review profiles, marketplace listings,
  case-study basics — bounded effort, compounding return, perennial Quick-wins.
- **Founder channels need founder appetite.** Personal LinkedIn, podcasting, speaking, hosting
  events — these die without sustained personal energy. Treat appetite as a gating fact
  (Needs-judgment), never assume it.
- **Paid is an amplifier, not a fix.** Paid spend on top of unclear positioning or a weak
  conversion surface burns money to learn nothing. Sequence paid after the organic surface
  converts.
- **Events are lumpy and cash-heavy — band on pipeline, not footfall.** A conference or dinner
  is measured on sourced/influenced pipeline and meetings booked, never on attendees, badge
  scans, or "great energy in the room". A single well-targeted roundtable often out-returns a
  headline sponsorship at ten times the cost. Treat the biggest events as a bet that needs a
  meeting plan *before* the spend, not a brand gesture.
- **AI SDR / agentic outbound is an execution layer, not a new demand source.** It scales
  whatever outbound motion already works — and scales a broken one just as fast. Band it on
  qualified-meeting quality and reply sentiment, never send volume, and treat deliverability
  and brand-safety as gating facts: a domain torched by an over-eager agent is expensive to
  undo. Adopt only once the underlying ICP and message are proven by hand.

## PLG / self-serve B2B SaaS

Weight toward: SEO (editorial + programmatic where the data supports unique pages), AEO/LLM
citation (compounding and still under-priced), lifecycle email (trial activation and
reactivation — usually the highest-leverage unmeasured surface), in-app messaging, free
tools/PLG surfaces, review sites once volume exists.

Be sceptical of: cold outbound (works only with tight ICP + strong attribution; values-misfit
common), broad paid social (long consideration, self-serve price points rarely sustain CAC),
large-format events and sponsorship before brand basics exist (webinars and hosted virtual
events are the cheaper, better-fit event bet at this stage).

Trap: 400k programmatic pages of non-buyer traffic can look like a thriving channel. Band on
*buyer* outcomes, not traffic.

## Sales-led / sales-assisted B2B

Weight toward: outbound with genuine ICP discipline (warm and intent/signal-based over pure
cold), founder-led narrative channels (LinkedIn, guesting) since deals are trust-led,
partnerships and channel/agency programmes (if customers cluster in agencies or integrators,
that cluster is distribution, not just revenue), case-study and proof engines, and intimate
in-person events (dinners, roundtables, exec briefings) where the buyer actually congregates.

Be sceptical of: programmatic SEO (volume rarely matches a narrow buyer), broad-reach social,
large conferences bought as pure brand presence without a pre-booked meeting plan, anything
whose metric is impressions.

Trap: confusing pipeline-source ("where did the deal come from") with deal-influence; sales-led
attribution lies in the opposite direction from PLG attribution.

## Developer / API-led

Weight toward: documentation as marketing, MCP/agent and marketplace directory listings
(near-zero cost, reaches the buyer who self-serves), SDK presence (PyPI/npm/GitHub),
llms.txt/AEO surfaces, technical content with working code, hackathons and developer meetups
(high-fit where builders congregate and try the product hands-on), community presence where the
developers already are.

Be sceptical of: gated content (developers bounce), paid almost entirely, LinkedIn-first
strategies, and glossy conference sponsorship over hands-on formats.

Trap: a developer surface that exists but is disconnected from measurement reads as "no
channel" in analytics while quietly converting at the highest rate in the business. Verify
the live surface before banding.

## Audience-led / creator / prosumer

Weight toward: one owned-audience channel (newsletter or YouTube or podcast — pick one and
overload it), repurposing systems around it, community, collabs/cross-promotion with adjacent
audiences.

Be sceptical of: running three content channels at once (the classic over-extension), paid
before the organic engine proves the content resonates.

Trap: platform-native vanity metrics; band on owned-audience growth (email list, subscribers)
and downstream conversion.

## Services / consultancy / agency

Weight toward: founder reputation channels (LinkedIn, guesting, speaking, hosting intimate
events), referral and alumni-network systems, a narrow authority content programme
(point-of-view essays over SEO volume), partnerships with adjacent practices.

Be sceptical of: SEO at volume, paid search (high-trust purchases rarely start at an ad),
outbound at scale (one good referral outranks a thousand cold emails).

Trap: treating every channel as always-on; services demand is lumpy, and the channel system
should be designed for consistent presence at low cost with surge capability, not constant
production.

## Stage modifiers

- **Pre-revenue / pre-launch:** the map is mostly Grey by definition. The Call should pick 2
  channels on motion-priors plus founder-fit, and define the 90-day evidence plan that lets
  the next map be banded on data.
- **Early revenue (finding repeatability):** weight channels that generate *learning* per pound
  — direct conversations, communities, founder channels, small in-person events. Park anything
  that needs scale to read (including AI SDR — automate a motion only once it's proven by hand).
- **Scaling (repeatable motion proven):** now concentration inverts slightly — protect the
  proven channels with owners and metrics, and run one structured experiment lane at a time
  for new ones.
- **Post-team-change (marketer departed, agency offboarded):** capacity is the binding
  constraint; re-band effort through the lens of who remains, and expect previously-Green
  production-dependent channels to soften — flag them before they fall.
