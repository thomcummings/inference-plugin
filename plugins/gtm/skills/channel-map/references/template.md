# Channel map — document template

The structure below is the canonical deliverable shape. Adapt family letters and rows to the
business; keep the legends, the two-register split, the card format, and The Call intact.

## How to read this

**Status** — is the channel switched on?

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Activated | Running today, with ongoing activity |
| 🟡 Partial | Assets/infrastructure exist but no coherent programme, or under-used |
| ⏸ Paused | Was run, currently dormant, could restart |
| 🛑 Sunsetting | Being deliberately wound down |
| ⚪ Not activated | Not running — wishlist, audit-surfaced, or newly added for completeness |

**Health (RAG)** — performance on current evidence ([evidence vintage]). Provisional.

| Band | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟩 Green | Performing well / above stage-weight |
| 🟨 Amber | Working but softening, under-leveraged, or mixed signal |
| 🟥 Red | Underperforming, broken, or near-zero return |
| ⬜ Grey | No performance data to rate — not activated, or activated but unmeasured |

**Recommend** — derived from five lenses (evidence, prize, effort, fit, stage); every verdict
carries its because-line in the detail cards. `Monitor` marks passive/outcome rows where the
action is measurement, not building.

`Build-first` · `Next` · `Needs-judgment` · `Quick-win` · `Park/Skip` · `Monitor`

# Register 1 — Channels

## At a glance

One table per family. Default families — adapt as the business demands:

- **A. Search & SEO** (editorial SEO, programmatic pages, alt search engines)
- **B. AI & answer engines** (AEO/LLM citation, custom GPTs/agents)
- **C. Direct & brand** (direct/branded traffic — usually an outcome row → Monitor)
- **D. Email & lifecycle** (drips, broadcasts, acquisition newsletters, in-app messaging, push/SMS)
- **E. Owned media & content** (owned podcast, editorial/blog hub, owned newsletter/publication-as-media, guides/resource hub) — the audiences you publish to and own; distinct from D's lifecycle *mechanics* and N's video
- **F. Owned social** (per-platform; split founder personal vs company where both exist — LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, etc.)
- **G. Community** (Reddit, Slack/Discord, forums)
- **H. Earned media & PR** (link-building/digital PR, founder guesting, speaking slots, influencers / B2B creators)
- **I. Events & field marketing** (large conferences · intimate in-person events — dinners, roundtables, salons, exec briefings · hackathons · webinars & virtual events · meetups & user groups · trade shows & expos · owned/hosted events — your own summit, roadshow)
- **J. Advocacy, reviews & referral** (review sites, word of mouth, referral, ambassadors)
- **K. Partnerships & ecosystem** (integrations/marketplaces, co-marketing, partner programmes, affiliates)
- **L. Paid & sponsorships** (paid search, paid social, retargeting, review-site PPC, newsletter/podcast/event sponsorship)
- **M. Outbound** (cold email · cold LinkedIn/social outreach · warm outbound — prior engagement or connection · intent / signal-based outbound · AI SDR / agentic outbound)
- **N. Video** (YouTube, short-form)
- **O. Product-led & developer** (API/developer marketing, free tools, PLG surfaces)

Family overlaps to keep clean (split a row only where the *decision* differs):

- **Events (I) ↔ Paid (L) ↔ Earned (H).** The event *format* is the channel and lives in I. A pure paid placement you don't shape — a booth fee, a badge/logo sponsorship of someone else's event — bands under **L**. A speaking slot as an earned-visibility mechanism cross-refs **H**. One event can carry all three; split rows where the bet and the owner differ.
- **Owned media (E) ↔ Email (D) ↔ Video (N).** E is the *property and audience* (the podcast, the publication, the hub). D owns lifecycle *mechanics* (the sends, drips, deliverability). N owns video specifically. A newsletter-as-media (acquisition, audience-building) sits in E; the automated welcome sequence behind it sits in D.
- **Outbound (M): warm ↔ intent/signal-based.** These overlap heavily. If the trigger is a *prior relationship* (past engagement, a known connection), it's **warm (M3)**. If it's a *third-party or behavioural buying signal* on an otherwise-cold prospect, it's **intent/signal-based (M4)**. Collapse to one row unless the data source and the play genuinely differ.
- **AI SDR (M5)** is an execution layer, not a new demand source — an agent running cold/warm/intent motions. Keep it a distinct row only when it's a real programme with its own tooling and risk profile (deliverability, brand safety); otherwise note it inside the relevant M row.

## Per-channel detail

Each card follows: evidence → health basis → because → to investigate. Thin evidence by design
is fine — the gap *is* the finding.

**[ID]. [Channel name]** — [status] · [health] · [recommendation]
- Evidence: [cited facts with figures; citation keys point to the source key]
- Health basis: [one sentence — why this band on this evidence]
- Because: [one line through the lenses — e.g. "evidence moderate (one month) · prize high if
  fixed (reach proven) · effort low (tune, not build) · fit strained (founder dislikes
  outbound — flag) · stage ok"]
- To investigate: [the questions a deep-dive would answer; for Park rows, the reopen
  condition and the cheapest test that would generate it]

# Register 2 — Plays & assets

Initiatives that ride *across* channels and operate at campaign/franchise altitude — bigger
than any single channel's to-do list. They carry recommendations and feed the build list; they
are *not* banded for health (their carrier channels are).

**Cross-cutting test:** if a play rides on only one channel it's a *task* — move it into that
channel's detail card. Every row here should touch two or more families and name a
franchise-level outcome, not a fix.

| # | Play | Rides on | Recommend | Key open question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PL1 | [Franchise-level initiative — e.g. "organic-foundation reset", "data-report franchise", "flagship event + content franchise"] | [2+ families, e.g. A · B · O] | [band] | [the one question that gates it] |

*Single-channel tactical items (fix the canonical, claim a listing, surface proof on the deck)
do not belong here — they live inside their channel cards.*
