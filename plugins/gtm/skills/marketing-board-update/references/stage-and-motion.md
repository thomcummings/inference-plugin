# Stage & GTM motion overlays

Two axes shift what a board wants to hear: how mature the company is (stage) and how
it actually grows (motion). Adjust emphasis and vocabulary on both. The default
assumption throughout the skill is **sales-led / pipeline-driven B2B**; if the motion
is different, swap the metric vocabulary using the motion section below.

## Stage emphasis

### Seed
Boards care more about *discovering a repeatable growth model* than about polished
volume. Emphasise:
- Signal quality over scale.
- Early evidence of repeatable demand.
- ICP discovery and messaging learning.
- Channel viability and early conversion benchmarks.
- Efficient use of limited budget.

### Series A
The story shifts from experimentation to system-building. Emphasise:
- Repeatability of pipeline creation.
- Channel concentration risk.
- Conversion consistency.
- Early unit economics.
- Segment prioritisation and the foundation for scaling.

### Series B
Marketing should read as a managed growth system with operating leverage. Emphasise:
- A scalable pipeline engine.
- Efficiency and payback.
- Mix of sourced vs influenced revenue.
- Segment performance and forecast confidence.
- Contribution to durable revenue growth, and the trade-offs in scaling spend,
  brand, and team structure.

## Motion overlays
Keep the five core questions; change the metric vocabulary so it matches how the
business really grows. Using pipeline/CAC language for a PLG company (or vice versa)
is the fastest way to look like you don't understand your own model.

### Sales-led / pipeline (default)
Pipeline sourced vs influenced, pipeline coverage vs target, CAC, CAC payback, cost
per qualified opportunity, demo/meeting → opportunity → closed-won conversion, win
rate, ACV by segment, sales-cycle length.

### Product-led (PLG)
Signups and activation rate, time-to-value, product-qualified leads/accounts (PQL/
PQA), free-to-paid conversion, expansion/NRR, blended CAC vs LTV, viral/referral
coefficient. Marketing's board story is about feeding *and* converting self-serve
demand, and where sales-assist begins.

### Community / content-led
Audience and owned-reach growth *tied to* pipeline or signups, branded search trend,
share of voice in the category, inbound share of pipeline, cost per pipeline pound
vs paid, compounding vs paid dependency. Guard against vanity-reach framing — always
connect reach to commercial throughput.

### Marketplace / two-sided
Report both sides and the balance between them: supply and demand acquisition cost,
liquidity (match/fill rate), take rate, cohort retention per side, and which side is
the current constraint. The board wants to know marketing is easing the binding
constraint, not just adding volume to the abundant side.
