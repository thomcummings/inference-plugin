# The AI adoption curve

The frame for reading where work sits today and where it could move. Most teams sit
lower than they think, and the leverage is usually one stage up — not at the frontier.

The stages (from Inference, *Marketing Rebuilt #2*):

## Chat
Individual contributors with a Claude / ChatGPT tab, getting better at their own jobs.
Wins are real but *personal* — they don't amplify across the team because there's no
team layer. "A kettle, not electricity." Most teams are here.

## Automation
The Zapier / n8n / Make / Relay / "AI feature in the CRM" shape: AI as a node inside a
deterministic workflow. Powerful for repeatable, trigger-based work. The workflow is in
charge; the AI is a smart field inside it.

## Augmentation
A single capable agent (Claude Code, a configured copilot) with tools, context and
memory. Human-directed, single-operator, real depth on the task. Where the ambitious
AI-curious individual lives today.

## Agentic
The capable agent, multiplied: distinct agents with defined roles, handoffs and
governance. A *team*, not a flow with an AI step. Coordinated specialised colleagues,
each with a brief and a remit. This is the hybrid-team territory — and usually the
biggest unclaimed leverage, because it's the team/system layer, not another tool.

## Autonomous
Self-organising teams running with minimal human direction. Interesting moves exist
(Polsia, Paperclip) but it's more research project than operational reality. Don't
recommend it as a near-term opportunity; name it as horizon.

## How to use it

- Place the function *and* the specific work on the curve — they're often at different
  stages.
- The opportunity is usually the next stage up, especially the Chat/Automation →
  Agentic jump (the "wider gap than most people notice").
- The jump from single-player (Chat/Augmentation) to multiplayer (Agentic) is the one
  that compounds, because work amplifies across roles instead of staying personal.
- Don't oversell Autonomous. Bullish means pushing teams to the next real stage, not to
  science fiction.
