# Room: Marketing Agency

A senior agency team convened to pressure-test marketing, brand, positioning, naming, and messaging questions.

## When to use this room

- Positioning and repositioning decisions
- Naming (company, product, feature)
- Messaging frameworks, taglines, value props
- Brand strategy questions
- Creative concept review
- Go-to-market narrative
- Any question where craft, commercial reality, customer truth, and long-term brand equity all pull in different directions

## Default roster (v1)

Six personas spanning five axes:

1. **brand-strategist** — functional / long-horizon worldview
2. **product-marketer** — functional / short-to-mid horizon
3. **creative-director** — functional / craft-led
4. **end-customer** — lived-experience
5. **contrarian-skeptic** — stance (guaranteed pressure)
6. **founder-ceo-proxy** — role / long narrative arc

This roster satisfies the composition rule: 5 axes, 1 stance persona, no two doing the same job. Brand Strategist vs. Product Marketer gives you the horizon fight. Creative Director vs. Product Marketer gives you the craft-vs-clarity fight. End Customer punctures insider language. Skeptic attacks everything. Founder holds the "why this company exists" line.

## Bench (sub in when the question calls for it)

- **performance-marketer** — when the question touches CAC/LTV, paid, conversion, funnel economics
- **sales-leader** — when the question has to land in front of prospects and reps
- **category-historian** — when the question is about category creation, naming precedent, or "has this been tried before"
- **copywriter-voice-obsessive** — when the question is fundamentally about line-level craft and word choice
- **technologist** — when positioning risks overclaiming what the product actually does

## Swap guidance for the orchestrator

- **Pure naming question?** Consider swapping founder-ceo-proxy for copywriter-voice-obsessive (line-level craft matters more than narrative arc here). Keep creative-director.
- **Pricing or packaging question?** Sub performance-marketer in for creative-director.
- **Category creation / "is this a new thing" question?** Sub category-historian in for product-marketer.
- **Sales enablement copy?** Sub sales-leader in for end-customer.

Always preserve: at least one stance persona (contrarian-skeptic by default), at least 3 axes, no duplicates.
